Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The "Daily Ganbatte Publisher" app is used only by the owner of Daily Ganbatte! to upload our own videos to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What we access

When the owner signs in, the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile information (account ID and username) for the owner's own account. We use these only to upload videos and check that uploads succeeded.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share data about viewers or any other users. We do not run ads through the app or track visitors to this site. This site sets no cookies.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted and used only by our automated upload process. They are deleted if the owner disconnects the app.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok or Instagram settings. To ask about any data, email sharkhity5@gmail.com.
